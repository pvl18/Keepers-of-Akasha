# PRE-EVENT-ASSETS

This file declares the materials and resources our team had access to
before development began at the hackathon.

## Team-created materials

Before the event, our team had:

-   Our submitted solution/presentation document for **Keepers of
    Akasha**.
-   Our submitted **AgentSpec**, describing the proposed problem, scope,
    agent workflow, and intended behaviour.
-   Prior discussion and planning with **ChatGPT** and **Claude** while
    exploring, refining, and understanding the project idea.

## Organizer-provided materials

Before the event, we also had access to materials provided by the
hackathon organizers, including:

-   The **AgentSpec template**.
-   A **sample AgentSpec** provided as a reference.
-   Feedback provided on our submitted presentation/solution.
-   Other event guidance and reference material supplied to participants
    before the hackathon.

These materials were used as guidance for understanding the problem,
preparing the specification, and refining the proposed solution.

## What we did not bring

We did not bring a pre-built implementation of Keepers of Akasha, an
existing project codebase, a custom dataset, or a pre-built evaluation
set.

The implementation used for the hackathon was developed during the
event.
